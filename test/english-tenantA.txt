At **[Company Name]**, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you’re not happy with your order, we offer a hassle-free return and refund policy.  

### **Eligibility for Returns**  
- Items must be returned within **30 days** from the date of delivery.  
- Products should be **unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging** with all tags and labels intact.  
- Certain items, such as **personal care products, customized goods, and perishable items**, are non-returnable for hygiene and safety reasons.  

### **How to Initiate a Return**  
1. Log in to your account and navigate to the **Orders** section.  
2. Select the item you want to return and submit a return request.  
3. Pack the item securely and use the provided return label for shipping.  
4. Once we receive the returned item, it will be inspected within **3-5 business days**.
